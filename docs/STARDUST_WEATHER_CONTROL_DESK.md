## Non-claims

ANCAP does **not**:

- operate a live geoengineering / weather remote-control console;
- unilaterally modify weather worldwide;
- issue official meteorological warnings;
- guarantee rainfall, storm dissipation, or climate outcomes;
- claim affiliation with StardustSRT unless a separate partner contract exists.

AccuWeather on the Earth widget remains indicative UX only (`/legal/market-data`).
