# Research references — Daewoong eTurna mRNA LNP (USPTO notice of allowance)

ANCAP cites **public journalism** about a USPTO notice of allowance for ionizable lipids used in Daewoong Pharmaceutical’s **eTurna** lipid-nanoparticle (LNP) platform. This is educational context for AETERNA licensed-partner consult workflows.

**Legal as of:** 11 September 2026.

## What was reported

- **Announced:** 27 August 2026 (company / Korean press); RU coverage 8 September 2026.
- **Office action:** USPTO **notice of allowance** — the application passed examination; remaining formalities are required before a U.S. patent is issued and enforceable.
- **Cited title:** *Lipid Structures and Compositions Comprising the Same* (press wording; *Comprising Same* also appears).
- **Subject:** ionizable lipid structures that form LNPs to carry therapeutic nucleic acids such as mRNA.
- **Use case in coverage:** deliver mRNA encoding **partial** cellular-reprogramming factors (Epigenetic Reprogramming of Aging / **ERA**) so aged cells regain some youthful functions **without** becoming stem cells.
- **Assets:** eTurna / ERA IP described as acquired from **Turn Biotechnologies** (May 2026 coverage); HanAll Biopharma licensing also mentioned in press.
- **Stage:** preclinical (human dermal fibroblasts / aged-tissue assays in the patent specification, per press). **Not** an approved anti-aging drug.
