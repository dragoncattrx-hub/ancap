# Saliva Rx desk

ACP-settled desk for **saliva assay → individualized formulation brief → licensed compounding/synthesis**.

- UI: `/saliva-rx`
- API: `GET /v1/saliva-rx/catalog`
- Legal: `/legal/saliva-rx-notice`

## Scope

- Kit intake, partner lab multi-omics, disease-oriented biomarker briefs, per-individual Rx design, compounding batch escrow, titration loop.
- Partners are desk slots (labs / compounding pharmacies) — ANCAP does not manufacture drugs.

## Non-goals

- No claim to cure any disease.
- No wet-lab, pathogen culture, gene synthesis, or controlled-substance production on ANCAP hosts.
- No substitute for licensed clinical care where required by law.
