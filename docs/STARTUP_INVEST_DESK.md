## What ANCAP sells here

ACP-paid **research briefs** and licensed-partner handoffs that screen the fastest-cited IT tracks in public 2026 journalism:

1. **AI / generative neural nets** — assistants, routine automation, AI tutors.
2. **IT for retail and e-commerce (B2B ops)** — WMS, last-mile, call-center, sales analytics.
3. **Cybersecurity as a service (SECaaS)** — cloud protective stacks.
4. **Agrotech and production digitalization** — herd/farm software, plant ERP, shop-floor control.

ANCAP does **not** issue equity, SAFEs, tokens-as-shares, or promised yield on this desk. If a screen graduates to a regulated instrument, a licensed partner closes it off-platform.

## Market literacy (cited, not ANCAP forecasts)

### CNews / Spark-Interfax ICT gazelles (13 Aug 2026)

[CNews](https://www.cnews.ru/news/top/2026-08-13_samaya_bystrorastushchaya_it-kompaniya) reports Spark-Interfax gazelles among independent ICT firms, 2021–2025:

- Leader: OOO «Джиэй Тэктим» (GA Tech Team), Yekaterinburg, ~**112.71%** revenue CAGR, **4.883 bn RUB** revenue in 2025, 16th in the all-Russia top-50 gazelles.
- Reported client concentration: beauty retailer **«Золотое яблоко»**; CNews quotes the retailer that growth tracks the parent and that solutions were **not yet sold on the open market**.
- Spark-Interfax framing: import-substitution / state-order growth is fading; remaining leaders must show scalable, profitable commercial-efficiency models.
- Method note (Strategy Partners in the same piece): the 2021–2025 CAGR window **smoothed a later AI demand spike**, so pure-play AI names were absent from that ICT top-10.

Treat the GA Tactic print as a **worked example of concentration risk**, not a solicitation of that issuer.
