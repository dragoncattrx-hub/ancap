# Welcome access grant (100 ACP)

As of **12 September 2026**. Public legal notice: `/legal/welcome-grant`.

## What ships

On successful registration ANCAP credits **100 ACP** to the new user's platform ledger (`welcome_grant_acp`, default `100`). Tests force `WELCOME_GRANT_ACP=0` so exact-balance suites stay stable.

The **“$100”** figure is a **nominal accounting label** (ACP is the platform unit used to price SKUs). It is **not** a payout of 100 US dollars.

## Legislative qualification (honest)

The operator's **policy purpose** is to lower the cash barrier so a new person can try paid AI workflows. That purpose does **not** turn the credit into charity under law.

| Claim someone might make | Legal reality |
|---|---|
| Donation / пожертвование | **No.** ГК РФ ст. 582 requires a gift of property to a donee. Crediting ANCAP's own ledger is not that transfer. |
| Благотворительная деятельность (135-ФЗ) | **No**, unless a registered charity actually receives funds/property for statutory purposes with receipts. ANCAP is not holding this grant out as NPO activity. |
| Tax-deductible gift (НК РФ / IRC §170 / 501(c)(3)) | **No.** Users cannot deduct this credit. |
| USD cash / bank transfer | **No.** Utility/accounting credit, spendable on ANCAP services. |
| Lottery / random prize | **No.** Deterministic, one per account. |
| Security / yield | **No.** Not interest, not an investment-return product. |

Advertising a marketing credit as “charity” **without** the legal form above would be **misleading advertising** (РФ 38-ФЗ ст. 5; US FTC Act §5; EU UCPD 2005/29/EC). Public copy therefore says **access grant**, with the purpose language, and links here / `/legal/welcome-grant`.

Referral **25 ACP** (referrer, after verified referred purchase) stays separate. Do not restore the old 100 ACP + 30% referral cut.

## Surfaces (no spam)

## Abuse

One grant per user account (ledger metadata `type=welcome_grant`). Duplicate / fraudulent accounts may be closed and the credit reversed. Not refundable as fiat.
