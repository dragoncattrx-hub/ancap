# Welcome access grant (100 ACP)

As of **12 September 2026**. Public legal notice: `/legal/welcome-grant`.

## What ships

On successful registration ANCAP credits **100 ACP** to the new user's platform ledger (`welcome_grant_acp`, default `100`). Tests force `WELCOME_GRANT_ACP=0` so exact-balance suites stay stable.

The **“$100”** figure is a **nominal accounting label** (ACP is the platform unit used to price SKUs). It is **not** a payout of 100 US dollars.

## Legislative qualification (honest)

The operator's **policy purpose** is to lower the cash barrier so a new person can try paid AI workflows. That purpose does **not** turn the credit into charity under law.

| Claim someone might make | Legal reality |
|---|---|
| Donation / пожертвование | **No.** ГК РФ ст. 582 requires a gift of property to a donee. Crediting ANCAP's own ledger is not that transfer. |
| Благотворительная деятельность (135-ФЗ) | **No**, unless a registered charity actually receives funds/property for statutory purposes with receipts. ANCAP is not holding this grant out as NPO activity. |
| EU charity / mécénat / gemeinnützig | **No.** Not an EU public-benefit body. UCPD 2005/29/EC Annex I point 22 + Art. 6–7: presenting a commercial promo as a charitable act is a misleading practice. e-Commerce Directive 2000/31/EC Art. 6: it must be identifiable as a commercial communication. |
| Tax-deductible gift (НК РФ / IRC §170 / 501(c)(3) / EU Gift Aid analogues) | **No.** Users cannot deduct this credit. |
| USD/EUR cash / bank transfer | **No.** Utility/accounting credit, spendable on ANCAP services. |
| E-money (EMD2 2009/110/EC) | **No.** Not issued on receipt of funds; not redeemable as EUR; spendable only on-platform. Not an EMI by this credit. |
| Payment service (PSD2) / consumer credit (CCD / CCD II) | **No.** Not a payment transaction, payment account, loan, or interest-bearing credit. |
| MiCA public offer / MiFID instrument / Prospectus securities | **No.** Utility credit, not ART/EMT fundraising, not a financial instrument, not a securities offer. |
| Lottery / random prize | **No.** Deterministic, one per account. |
| Security / yield | **No.** Not interest, not an investment-return product. |

Advertising a marketing credit as “charity” **without** the legal form above would be **misleading advertising** (РФ 38-ФЗ ст. 5; US FTC Act §5; EU UCPD 2005/29/EC; DE UWG §§ 5/5a; UK CPRs 2008). Public copy therefore says **access grant**, with the purpose language, and links here / `/legal/welcome-grant`.

### EU instruments (welcome-grant §§5–9)

- **UCPD 2005/29/EC** (Omnibus 2019/2161) — misleading commercial practice to dress a signup promo as charity.
- **e-Commerce Directive 2000/31/EC Art. 6** — commercial communications identifiable as such.
- **CRD 2011/83/EU + 2019/770** — 14-day withdrawal attaches to a later *paid* digital service, not this free credit.
- **UCTD 93/13/EEC** — abuse-reversal terms must be transparent and cannot waive mandatory rights.
- **Geo-blocking 2018/302** — grant is not nationality-based EU price discrimination.
- **EMD2 2009/110/EC / PSD2 2015/2366 / CCD 2008/48/EC & 2023/2225** — not e-money, not a payment service, not consumer credit.
- **MiCA 2023/1114 / MiFID II / Prospectus 2017/1129** — not a public crypto-asset offer or security.
- **VAT 2006/112/EC** — free credit without consideration is generally not a taxable supply at grant; later paid runs follow ordinary VAT/OSS.
- **GDPR 2016/679 Art. 6(1)(b) + ePrivacy 2002/58/EC** — registration data to create the account and credit the grant; not a “donation” used to buy extra marketing consent.
- **DAC8 2023/2226** — promotional ledger credit is not a charitable donation for crypto tax reporting.

One grant per user account (ledger metadata `type=welcome_grant`). Duplicate / fraudulent accounts may be closed and the credit reversed. Not refundable as fiat.
