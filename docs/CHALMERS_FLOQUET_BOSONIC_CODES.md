# Research references — Chalmers Floquet bosonic codes

ANCAP cites **public journalism and the PRL record** for a theoretical method that implements bosonic-code operations with **quantum lattice gates in a single Floquet period** instead of thousands of adiabatic drive cycles.

**Legal as of:** 11 September 2026.

## What was reported

- **Paper:** Tangyou Huang, Lei Du, Lingzhen Guo, *Single-Period Floquet Control of Bosonic Codes with Quantum Lattice Gates*, Physical Review Letters **137**, DOI [10.1103/tnb8-3m8m](https://doi.org/10.1103/tnb8-3m8m) (published 3 August 2026).
- **Affiliations in the record:** Chalmers University of Technology (MC2); Tianjin University.
- **RU coverage:** [Nauka TV, 10 September 2026](https://naukatv.ru/news/najden_sposob_uskoreniya_kvantovykh_vychislenij_v_1000_raz).
- **Idea (literacy):** store information in **microwave / resonator bosonic codes** (not only lone transmons); use **quantum lattice gates** as a universal bosonic primitive set; **Floquet** periodic drive synthesizes the unitary in **one period**. Press frames this as ~**1000× fewer cycles** on that control axis (not a general “quantum computers are 1000× faster” product claim).
- **Stage:** theoretical. Authors discuss a future demonstration on Chalmers’ **developing 100-qubit superconducting** platform. Not an ANCAP machine.
